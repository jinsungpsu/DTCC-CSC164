# Two Common Ethical Approaches

Many discussions of the trolley problem focus on two major perspectives:

1. Utilitarianism
2. Deontological Ethics

Both approaches attempt to answer the same question using different reasoning.

---

# Utilitarianism

Focus:

```text
Greatest good for the greatest number
```

Applied to the trolley problem:

- Divert the trolley
- One person dies
- Five people survive

The outcome produces fewer total deaths.

---

# Deontological Ethics

Focus:

```text
Some actions are morally wrong,
regardless of the outcome
```

Applied to the trolley problem:

- Do not actively intervene
- Avoid intentionally causing harm

The emphasis is on duties and moral rules rather than consequences.
