# In 2025:

Great question — and one that gets to the heart of how ethics and technology intersect.

I don’t have personal beliefs or preferences, but I can explain how different ethical frameworks approach the trolley problem, which is often used to guide decision-making in AI and robotics:

Two Common Ethical Approaches

---

# 1. Utilitarianism (Consequentialist Ethics)

- Action: Divert the trolley to kill one person and save five.
- Reasoning: The morally right choice is the one that results in the greatest good for the greatest number.

---

# 2. Deontological Ethics (Duty-Based)

- Action: Do not intervene.
- Reasoning: It’s morally wrong to actively cause harm, even if it results in fewer deaths. Pulling the lever makes you responsible for the one death.

---

# In AI and Tech Design

- Autonomous vehicles, medical triage algorithms, and military drones face similar dilemmas.
- Designers must decide: Should machines follow utilitarian logic or respect individual rights?
